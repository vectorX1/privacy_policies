Trails is an Android expense tracker. It reads bank debit alerts in your SMS messages and turns them into expenses on your phone. This policy explains what Trails reads, what it saves and what it does with that information.

The short version

  • Your SMS and expenses stay on your phone. Trails does not send them to the developer or to anyone else.
  • Trails has no account, sign-in, ads or analytics.
  • Your data leaves your phone only if you export or back it up to a location you choose.

SMS messages Trails reads

If you allow it, Trails checks incoming SMS messages on your phone to find bank debit alerts. If you run Scan SMS, it also reads your SMS inbox for the dates you choose, to find debits it missed.

Messages that don't look like debit alerts, such as personal messages and one-time passwords, are ignored and are not saved.

Information saved on your phone

For each debit alert, Trails saves the details needed to show the expense:

  • Amount, date and time, merchant or UPI ID, and category
  • Bank name, SMS sender ID, payment type and reference number
  • A masked account number showing only the last digits

Full SMS text: if "Ask before saving every debit" is on, Trails keeps the text of the message only while the expense waits for your review. The text is deleted when you save or ignore that expense. If the setting is off, the text is not saved.

Ignored items: Trails keeps the details of an expense you ignore, without the SMS text, so a later scan doesn't add it again.

Other information: Trails also saves expenses and notes you enter, the categories and rules it learns from your edits, your senders list, and your settings, such as your budget and theme.

You can turn off capture in Trails or remove these permissions in your phone's settings at any time. You can still add expenses by hand.

Sharing

Trails does not sell or share your information. The app does not have internet permission and does not upload your SMS or expenses.

You control two ways of moving data off the app:

  • CSV export: creates an unencrypted file of your expenses. It does not include SMS text.
  • Entire app backup: creates a file encrypted with a passphrase you choose. It does not include SMS text or items waiting for review. The passphrase can't be recovered if you lose it.

These files are saved where you choose. If you choose a cloud service or share a file, that service's or recipient's own practices apply. Android's automatic app backup is turned off for Trails.

Google Play

If you install Trails from Google Play, Google handles the download and may collect information under its own terms, as described in the Google Privacy Policy. Google may also give the developer summary statistics, such as install numbers and crash reports. Trails does not send your SMS or expenses to Google.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays on your phone until you delete it. The developer has no copy, so the developer can't view, change or delete it for you.

  • Delete an expense: open it in Trails and delete it.
  • Delete everything: go to Settings, then Reset & delete all data. Your theme and the record that you finished setup and the tour are kept.
  • Remove the app: uninstalling Trails deletes its data from your phone.

Files you exported or backed up are not deleted by these steps. Delete them separately.

Security

Trails keeps your information in its private storage on your phone. Anyone who can unlock and use your phone may be able to open the app, so use a screen lock. No method of storage is completely secure.

Children

Trails is meant for adults managing their own finances. It is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If Trails changes how it handles information, this page and its effective date will be updated. If a new feature uses internet access or accepts payments, this policy will be updated to describe it before that feature is released.
