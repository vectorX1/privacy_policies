Eliva Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026
App: Eliva: blood pressure and glucose log (Android, package com.eliva.app)
Developer: Utilio Apps
Contact: utilio.apps@gmail.com

Summary

  • Eliva keeps your health readings only on your phone.
  • Eliva does not ask for internet access, so it cannot send your data anywhere.
  • There is no account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • We (the developer) never receive, see, store, share or sell any of your data.
  • Data leaves your phone only when you choose to share a report, an export or a backup file.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy explains how the Eliva Android app handles information. It covers the app only. It does not cover other apps or services you choose to use with Eliva, such as the email, messaging, cloud storage or printing app you pick when sharing a file.

2. Information Eliva stores on your phone

Eliva stores the following information locally on your device, in the app's private storage. Other apps cannot read it.

Health readings you enter

  • Blood pressure: systolic, diastolic and pulse.
  • Blood glucose, with its unit (mg/dL or mmol/L) and context (for example fasting, after meal, bedtime) and hours since eating, if you add them.
  • The date and time of each reading.
  • Optional details: arm, body position, tags (such as after medicine, stress, caffeine or after exercise) and a free-text note.

Settings and preferences

  • What you track (blood pressure, glucose or both).
  • Your target ranges.
  • Reminder routines and times.
  • Appearance choices: theme, text size, language and the sections shown on Home.
  • Whether App lock is on.
  • The name you choose to print on doctor reports, if you enter one. This is optional.

What Eliva does not collect

Eliva does not collect or store:

  • your name (other than the optional report name above), email address, phone number or any account details
  • your location
  • your contacts, photos, media or other files
  • device identifiers, advertising IDs or usage statistics
  • crash reports or diagnostic logs sent to anyone
  • your fingerprint, face data, PIN, pattern or password

3. How Eliva uses your information

Your information is used only to provide the app's features on your phone:

  • showing your readings, history and trends
  • checking each reading against the ranges you set
  • working out averages, counts and weekly insights
  • sending the reminders you set up
  • creating PDF reports, PDF tables and CSV files when you ask for them
  • creating and restoring backup files when you ask for them

Eliva does not use your information for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than these features.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not share, sell, rent or trade your information with anyone. We do not receive it in the first place.

Your data leaves your phone only when you choose to:

  • share or save a doctor report (PDF)
  • download your readings as a PDF table or CSV file
  • create and share a backup file (.eliva)

When you do this, Android shows you a list of apps or locations to choose from. The file goes only to the app or place you pick. Once the file is outside Eliva, it is handled by that app or service under its own privacy policy, and anyone you send it to can read it. Please share health information only with people and services you trust.

6. Backups

  • Android cloud backup is switched off for Eliva. Your readings are not copied to Google Drive or any other cloud backup by the system, and they are not moved by device-to-device transfer.
  • You can make your own backup from Settings > Backup and restore. This creates a single .eliva file that contains your readings and settings.
  • Password protection is optional.
    • With a password, the file is encrypted on your phone with AES-256-GCM, using a key derived from your password (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with a random salt). Without the password, the file cannot be read. The password is not stored anywhere, and we cannot recover it. If you forget it, the backup cannot be restored.
    • Without a password, the file is not encrypted, and anyone who gets the file can read your readings.
  • You decide where the backup file is stored (for example your phone's storage, a USB drive or a cloud storage app you trust).
  • Restoring a backup replaces the data in the app. Eliva shows you what will change before you confirm.

7. Data security

  • All data is kept in Eliva's private app storage, which Android isolates from other apps.
  • Eliva has no internet permission, so it cannot transmit your data over a network.
  • The optional App lock asks for your fingerprint or phone screen lock when you open Eliva, and locks again when you leave the app.
  • Optional password-protected backups are encrypted as described above.

No method of storage is perfectly secure. Your phone's own protection (screen lock, encryption and keeping the system up to date) is an important part of keeping your data safe.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • Your data stays on your phone until you delete it.
  • You can delete a single reading, several readings, or all readings at any time from within the app. Every delete asks for confirmation.
  • Uninstalling Eliva, or clearing its storage in Android settings, permanently deletes all of its data from the phone.
  • Report, export and backup files you saved or shared outside the app are not deleted by Eliva. You can delete them from wherever you put them.

Because we never receive your data, we have nothing to delete on our side, and we cannot recover data you have deleted.

9. Your rights and choices

Because all data stays on your device and under your control, you can at any time:

  • see all your data in the app (Home, Log and Trends)
  • change any reading or setting
  • export your data as PDF, CSV or a backup file
  • delete any or all of your data
  • withdraw permissions (camera, notifications, alarms) in Android settings. The related feature stops working, and the rest of the app keeps working.

If you live in a region with data protection laws (such as the GDPR in the EU/UK, the DPDP Act in India, or US state privacy laws), these rights apply. As the developer does not process or hold your personal data, requests to access or delete data held by us will find none. You can exercise all of these rights directly in the app.

10. Children

Eliva is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from children, and the app does not send any information to us. A parent or guardian may use Eliva to record a child's readings, and that data stays on their phone.

11. Not a medical device

Eliva is a logbook. It does not measure blood pressure or glucose, diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, or give medical advice. Readings are entered by you, or read from your own monitor's display with Scan, and compared with ranges you set. Always talk to a qualified healthcare professional about your health and your readings.

12. Changes to this policy

If a future version of Eliva ever changes how data is handled (for example by adding an online feature), we will update this policy before that version is released and explain the change in the app.
